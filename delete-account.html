Delete Your Account

Ebonforge: Path to Godhood

How to Request Deletion

Send us an email from the Google account you linked in the game. You do not need to install the game to make this request.

Email: zkyxgame@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my account

We use the email address you write from to find your account, so please send it from the same Google account you linked in the game.

What to Include

If you have it, include your Player ID. It helps us find your account faster. Your Player ID is shown at the top of the Settings screen in the game.

What We Delete

  • Your cloud save backups
  • Your account record and its link to your Google account
  • Backup history and any information stored with your backups

What Stays on Your Phone

Your game progress is also saved on your phone. Deleting your account does not remove it, so you can keep playing offline. Uninstalling the game removes the save from your phone.

What We Keep

We only keep information we are legally required to keep, such as:

  • Purchase records needed for tax, accounting and refund purposes
  • Payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Processing Time

We process deletion requests within 30 days and reply by email when it is done.

Delete Your Account in the Game

You can also delete your account and cloud data yourself, straight away:

  1. Open the game
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Find Account & Cloud Backup
  4. Tap Delete account & data and confirm

This cannot be undone.

Delete Your Account

Ebonforge: Path to Godhood

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account, please send an email request from the Google account you linked in the game.

Email Address: [your address]

Subject: Delete my account

What to Include

In your email, please include your Player ID if you have it. You can find this in:

  1. Open the game
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Player ID

What We Delete

  • Your cloud backups
  • Your account record

What We Keep

We retain anything we're legally required to keep, such as:

  • Purchase records
  • Other legally required information

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Delete Account In-Game

You can also delete your account and all game data directly from the app:

  1. Open the game
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Account & Cloud Backup
  4. Choose Delete account & data

If you have any questions, please contact us via email.

Delete Your Account

How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account and game data in Ebonforge: Path to Godhood, you have two options:

Option 1: Email Request

Send an email from the Google account you linked in the game:

Email Address: zkyxgame@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my account

Include: Your Player ID (found in Settings → Player ID)

Option 2: In-Game Deletion

Delete your account and all game data directly from the app:

  1. Open the game
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Account & Cloud Backup
  4. Choose Delete account & data

What Gets Deleted

  • Your cloud backups
  • Your account record
  • All game progress and data

What We Keep

We retain information we're legally required to keep, including:

  • Purchase records (for tax and compliance)
  • Other legally mandated information

Processing Time

For email requests: Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

For in-game deletion: Your account is deleted immediately upon confirmation.

Questions?

If you have any questions or issues with account deletion, please contact us:

Email: zkyxgame@gmail.com

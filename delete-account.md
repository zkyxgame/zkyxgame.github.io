# Ebonforge: Path to Godhood - Delete Your Account

## How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account, please email your request to [your address] from the Google account you linked in the game.

**Subject line:** "Delete my account"

**In your email:** Include your Player ID (found in Settings → Player ID) if you have it.

## What We Delete

- Your cloud backups
- Your account record

## What We Keep

- Anything we're legally required to keep, such as purchase records

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are handled within 30 days.

## In-Game Deletion Option

You can also delete everything directly in the game:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Select **Account & Cloud Backup**
3. Choose **Delete account & data**
